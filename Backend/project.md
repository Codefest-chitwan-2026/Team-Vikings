# 🍽️sharePlate

> **Every Meal Matters.**

sharePlate
 is a social impact platform that combats food waste by connecting food businesses with NGOs and nearby consumers through a two-stage surplus food distribution model. The platform ensures that edible surplus food reaches people instead of landfills by prioritizing donations and, if unclaimed, converting them into affordable flash-sale deals.

Designed for Nepal, sharePlate
 aims to create a sustainable ecosystem where businesses reduce waste, communities receive nutritious food, and consumers gain access to quality meals at discounted prices.

---

# 📌 Problem Statement

Every day, hotels, restaurants, cafés, bakeries, and party palaces discard large amounts of perfectly edible surplus food due to overproduction, canceled events, or unsold inventory. At the same time, NGOs, orphanages, elderly homes, and low-income communities struggle with food insecurity.

The lack of a centralized platform to efficiently redistribute surplus food results in:

- Massive food waste
- Financial loss for businesses
- Increased environmental impact
- Missed opportunities to feed communities in need

sharePlate
 bridges this gap through a smart surplus food redistribution network.

---

# 💡 Solution

sharePlate
 introduces a **dual-track surplus food distribution system**.

## 🟢 Track 1 — NGO Priority Donation

Businesses can instantly publish surplus food as a free donation.

Verified NGOs, orphanages, and elderly homes receive priority access and can claim the food within a predefined time window (e.g., 45 minutes).

If claimed:

- NGO confirms pickup
- QR verification ensures secure handover
- Food reaches communities before spoilage

---

## 🟠 Track 2 — Flash Marketplace

If no NGO claims the donation before the countdown expires, the listing is automatically converted into a discounted flash sale.

Nearby consumers can:

- Discover discounted meals
- Reserve food instantly
- Pick it up before the expiry window closes

This helps businesses recover part of their costs while preventing perfectly edible food from becoming waste.
